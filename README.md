RNA binding project
=====
Protein of interest [ELAVL1](https://www.encodeproject.org/genes/1994/)

ENCODE API: https://www.encodeproject.org/help/rest-api/

ELAVL1 encodes the ELAV-like RNA-binding protein 1 and acts as a shuttling RNA-binding protein that stabilizes target mRNAs. It selectively binds AU-rich and poly-U elements in the 3'-UTR of transcripts and promotes mRNA stability and translation, linking post-transcriptional control to gene expression. Its best-studied targets include MYC, FOS, and leptin, and it is implicated in embryonic stem cell differentiation.

ELAVL1 contains multiple RNA recognition motifs and localizes to the cytoplasm, nucleus, cytosol, and nucleoplasm. Through binding of AU-rich elements, it counteracts degradation signals in mRNAs and thereby supports transcript persistence. It also preferentially stabilizes non-m6A-methylated transcripts, while binding m6A-modified MYC mRNA to enhance its stability. Expression is high in the endocrine system, respiratory system, and gastrointestinal tract, with additional expression in hematopoietic, female reproductive, and male reproductive tissues.
